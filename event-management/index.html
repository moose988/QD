Questions about QD Events

Which celebrations can QD Events be used for?

The platform has wedding and engagement and general celebration categories. Invitations can be designed for birthdays, gender reveals, graduations and other occasions; dedicated workflows for each type are not implied.

Can the invitation be personalized?

Yes. The invitation design and event details can be prepared around your celebration. The exact design and content are agreed as part of the project.

How do guests receive their invitations?

Each invitation can be shared with its guest using a link. The event host chooses how to send or share that link. Automated WhatsApp delivery is not included as a platform claim.

Can guests RSVP and bring additional guests?

Guests can confirm or decline online. The host can manage the additional-guest allowance associated with each invitation and review replies in the event workspace.

Can I plan seating and check in guests?

The dashboard supports table and seat planning, guest seat visibility, and QR-enabled guest check-in tools.

Are invitations available in all four website languages?

The QD Systems product page is available in English, Arabic, Simplified Chinese and Russian. Invitation templates have their own language options; available languages vary by template and are confirmed during planning.

How can I ask about an event?

Book a free call through QD Systems or message us on WhatsApp. We can discuss the celebration, invitation direction and event-management needs.
