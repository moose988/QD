AI chat and automation questions

Common questions about AI chatbots and workflow automation for businesses in the UAE.

What can an AI chatbot do for a business?

A chatbot can answer common questions, capture lead details or help a visitor request a booking using configured information and workflows. Suitable tasks and handoffs should be agreed for the project.

Can a chatbot support WhatsApp or booking enquiries?

QD Systems offers WhatsApp flows, lead capture, AI chat and booking. Integrations and the exact enquiry flow depend on the business’s tools and agreed scope.

Does an AI chatbot replace my team?

No. The workflow is planned so the assistant handles agreed tasks and a person can take over the cases it cannot resolve. Handoff points are defined during scoping, before anything is built.

How do you make business automation useful?

Start with a repeated task or handoff, clarify required information and recipients, then agree the steps and exceptions. A person should handle cases the workflow cannot resolve.

Can I try an AI assistant before starting a project?

Yes. The QD Systems website has a live assistant demo you can try to get a feel for an AI chat experience before discussing your own use case.
