AI chat and automation questions

What can an AI chatbot do for a business?

A business chatbot can answer common customer questions, capture lead details or help a visitor request a booking, based on the information and workflow it is configured to use. The suitable tasks and handoff to a person should be agreed as part of the project.

Can a chatbot support WhatsApp or booking enquiries?

QD Systems describes WhatsApp flows, lead capture, AI chat and booking as part of its offer. Available integrations and the exact enquiry or booking flow depend on the business's current tools and the agreed project scope.

How do you make business automation useful?

Start with a repeated task or handoff, clarify what information is needed and who should receive it, then agree the automation steps and exceptions before building. A person should remain able to handle cases the workflow cannot resolve.
