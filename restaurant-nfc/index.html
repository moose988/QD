One tap, connected to a useful next step

Restaurants, cafés and other hospitality businesses can use NFC tags at a table, counter or entrance to make a digital touchpoint easier to reach. QD Systems helps decide what each tag should open, then connects the NFC experience to the venue's chosen page or flow.

Ordering and service

How a restaurant NFC setup comes together

The aim is to make the tap predictable for guests and manageable for the venue. The destination and placement should match how people actually use the space.

Choose the guest action

Decide what the tap should open: a menu, order flow, service request, loyalty destination, review page or another web action.

Map the placement

Identify whether the tag belongs at each table, on the counter or somewhere else in the customer journey.

Connect the destination

QD Systems connects the tag to the agreed digital destination and considers the information a guest should see after tapping.

Test the experience

Check the tag and destination on compatible phones, then confirm the path is understandable in the venue's setting.

Restaurant NFC questions

What can an NFC tag do for a restaurant or café?

A tap can open a digital menu, ordering or loyalty experience, a table-service request, a review page or another agreed web action. The destination is chosen to fit the venue's customer journey.

Do customers need to install an app to use the NFC menu?

The QD Systems site describes its NFC tags as opening a digital experience without requiring a customer app. A compatible phone with NFC enabled can open the linked web destination.

Can different tables link to different actions?

QD Systems describes customizable tags and actions for different business needs. During scoping, the venue can discuss whether table-specific destinations or a shared destination are appropriate.
