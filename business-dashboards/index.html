Business dashboard questions

Common questions about custom dashboards, booking, order tracking and portals for UAE businesses.

What is a custom business dashboard?

It is an interface planned around the business information and tasks a team needs to see or manage. The scope depends on workflow, data sources, access needs and decisions the dashboard should support.

Can a dashboard connect with booking or order tracking?

QD Systems offers booking systems, order tracking, portals and dashboards. Which parts can be connected depends on existing tools and project scope.

Do we need to replace the tools we already use?

Not necessarily. Scoping reviews the tools and data sources a team already uses. Which of them a new system works alongside or connects with is agreed for the project.

How do you scope a dashboard project?

QD Systems discusses the process, information and user actions the system should support, then agrees the scope and quote in writing before work begins.

How much does a custom dashboard or business system cost?

Cost depends on scope: the workflow, the number of users and views, the information sources and any connections required. QD Systems agrees the scope and quote in writing before work begins.

Have a workflow to make clearer?

Describe what your team tracks, where the information lives and which tasks you want to support. QD Systems can discuss a custom dashboard or business system.
