Our Story

Trusted halal, from New York to the UAE

What began as a humble New York counter became a kitchen people trusted for one reason above all: the food was honest, generous, and made the right way.

It started with a small counter in New York — a family recipe, real charcoal, and a simple rule: never cut a corner on quality or on what halal means.

Two decades later, Hamza & Madina brings that same kitchen to the Emirates. Every cut of meat is fully halal-certified, every spice blend is made in-house, and every plate is cooked to order.
